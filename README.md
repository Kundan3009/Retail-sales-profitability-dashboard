# 📊 Retail Sales & Profitability Dashboard

An interactive Excel dashboard designed to analyze retail sales, profitability, monthly performance trends, and regional sales performance.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents a retail sales analysis dashboard created using Microsoft Excel. The dashboard converts sales data into meaningful KPIs, charts, and business insights to make performance analysis easier.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze total retail sales and profit
- Track monthly sales and profit trends
- Compare regional sales performance
- Monitor key business KPIs
- Identify the highest and lowest performing periods
- Present business insights through an easy-to-understand dashboard

|---|---:|

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### Monthly Sales & Profit Analysis

The dashboard compares sales and profit across:

- January
- February
- March

It uses a line chart to visualize monthly performance and identify changes in sales and profit.

### Regional Sales Analysis

Regional sales are presented for:

- North — ₹3,23,000
- South — ₹32,000
- East — ₹37,500
- West — ₹25,700

A bar chart makes it easier to compare sales across regions.

## 💡 Key Insights

- February recorded the highest monthly sales at ₹2,08,200.
- March recorded the lowest monthly sales at ₹12,000.
- North recorded the highest regional sales at ₹3,23,000.
- Total profit generated was ₹90,700.
- Overall profit margin was 21.69%.
